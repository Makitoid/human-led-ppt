## H. Academic / paper (rigorous / dense / defences and group meetings)
- Look: paper ground + serif headings + strict chart conventions; high density without noise. Good for thesis defence, lab meetings, technical review.
- Light: bg `#FBFAF7`, panel `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1A1A1A`, muted `#4D4D4D`, accent `#1F3A5F` (or departmental colour), warn `#8C2F1E`, rule `#D8D4CB`
- Dark: bg `#16181C`, panel `#1E2126`, ink `#E8E6E1`, muted `#A8A69F`, accent `#7FA8D9`, warn `#E08A6E`, rule `#33373E`
- Type: headings `"Source Han Serif SC","Noto Serif SC","Times New Roman",serif`; body `"Inter","PingFang SC","Microsoft YaHei",sans-serif`; figure captions 12–14px with numbered "Fig. 1 / Table 2" labels
- Rules: a kicker at the top of every page (section number + name); charts carry axis titles, units, n and year; footnotes as superscript numbers with a reference list on the last page; at most one accent colour plus greys
- Motion / transition: only "growth" animation in service of data — bars `width 0→final`, lines via `stroke-dashoffset`, table rows fading in at 150 ms; 300 ms cross-fade between pages, **no movement or scale** (flourish reads as unserious in academia).
- Helper skill: `agrimsingh-bringhurst-typography` (explicitly covers slides), `entur-accessibility` for WCAG 2.1; `cognitive-design` for chart load limits.
