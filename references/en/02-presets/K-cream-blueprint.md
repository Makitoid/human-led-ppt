## K. Cream blueprint (engineering · printable · zero gradients)
- Look: cream paper, a 48px blueprint grid at low alpha, 2px hard-bordered cards, one rust accent. Looks like a README you could pin to a wall.
- Signatures: ① grid backdrop (cover and architecture pages only) ② rust insight callout block on the right edge
- Default partner: H Academic's chart discipline, or A Fluent's order
- Light: bg `#F0EAE0`, panel `#F7F3EA`, ink `#1C1A16`(14.52), muted `#5A554C`(6.18), accent `#B5392A`(4.90), warn `#7A5A12`(5.32), rule `#C9BFAE`, accent-ink `#FFFFFF`(5.86)
- Dark: bg `#14171A`, panel `#1C2126`, ink `#E7E3D9`(14.04), muted `#A3A79F`(7.36), accent `#E4705D`(5.80), warn `#E0B95C`(9.65), rule `#2C333A`, accent-ink `#0B0B0B`(6.35)
- Type: body `Inter,"PingFang SC",sans-serif`; in-diagram labels and codenames always monospace; hero numerals may use a Playfair-class serif
- Fits: arch-diagram / flow / process-steps / table / verdict
- Motion: line-draw via `stroke-dashoffset`, boxes fading in; **zero shadows, zero-to-2px radius, zero gradients**
- Risk: 2px borders survive projectors better than 1px, but more than 4 cards per page turns the slide into graph paper; halve grid alpha on dark backgrounds
