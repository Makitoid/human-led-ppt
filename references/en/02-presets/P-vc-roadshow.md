## P. VC roadshow (traction · hero metrics · the ask)
- Look: white, navy→violet gradient confined to headings and the CTA, oversized KPIs, one traction curve.
- Signatures: ① a NOW/NEXT/LATER milestone rail per act ② a four-up KPI card row with 96–120px numerals and small delta chips
- Default partner: A Fluent or C OpenAI
- Light: bg `#FFFFFF`, panel `#F8FAFC`, ink `#0B1220`(18.72), muted `#46536B`(7.75), accent `#1F4FD8`(6.63), warn `#8A5A00`(5.93), rule `#E3E8F0`, accent-ink `#FFFFFF`(6.63)
- Dark: bg `#0B1020`, panel `#141B2E`, ink `#EEF2FA`(16.87), muted `#A6B0C4`(8.68), accent `#7FA7FF`(8.00), warn `#E8C05C`(10.93), rule `#222C44`, accent-ink `#0B0B0B`(8.32)
- Type: `Inter,"PingFang SC",sans-serif` 700 headings; every numeral `tabular-nums`
- Fits: kpi-grid / roadmap / chart-line / comparison / team / the-ask
- Motion: counting numerals (the shell's `data-count`) + line draw + card fades; **no entrance translation**
- Risk: this is the template that best hides an empty argument — every KPI must resolve to a row in the source file, and unsourced "trillion-dollar market" claims get deleted; gradients stay on headings/CTA, the body area stays panel-white
