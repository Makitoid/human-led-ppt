## Q. Macaron cards (lifestyle · slow rhythm · carousel)
- Look: cream base, three large blurred blobs, italic serif display mixed with sans body, rounded cards at high chroma but low glare (peach / mint / sky / lilac / lemon / rose).
- Signatures: ① ≤ 3 background blobs that only fade with the page (no drift) ② `Playfair italic` numerals `01–04`
- Default partner: I Playful's radius and pills, one weight and one notch of saturation lighter
- Light: bg `#FEF8F1`, panel `#FFFFFF`, ink `#2A2320`(14.64), muted `#6A6058`(5.81), accent `#8C5AA8`(4.81), warn `#946A12`(4.60), rule `#EFE2D4`, accent-ink `#FFFFFF`(5.07)
- Dark: bg `#1C1822`, panel `#26202F`, ink `#FBF3EA`(15.88), muted `#BEB2BE`(8.56), accent `#D6A6EE`(8.77), warn `#E8C05C`(10.08), rule `#38303F`, accent-ink `#0B0B0B`(9.89)
- Type: headings `"Playfair Display","Source Han Serif SC",serif` (italic only for numerals and pull quotes); regular sans body
- Fits: bullets (many cards) / image-gallery / chart-pie (donut) / quote / closing
- Motion: fade + 6px rise, 400–500ms, 120ms apart; no spring overshoot — it fights the softness
- Risk: **the tightest set in this table** — accent 4.81 and warn 4.60 sit barely above the 4.5 line, so re-measure after any tweak; more than 4 macaron cards per page is a candy shop, and the body area always stays panel-white
